1. Services

RightAlly will configure and deploy a dedicated instance of the RightAlly platform for you, and after the go-live date provide access to it on a monthly subscription.

2. Fees

The fees in the summary above form part of this agreement. The monthly fee is based on the number of agents on your account on each billing date, with a minimum of five.

3. Payment authorization

You authorize RightAlly and its payment processor, Stripe, to charge the payment method you provide for the deposit, the balance, each monthly fee and any amount due under Section 7, on their due dates.

4. Go-live date

The target go-live date is 30 days after signing. RightAlly may extend it by notifying you at least two days in advance. The balance is charged only on the go-live date as extended.

5. Term and renewal

The minimum term is 12 months from the go-live date. The agreement does not renew automatically. We will send you a renewal agreement at least 45 days before the term ends.

6. Non-payment and suspension

If a payment is still outstanding 30 days after its due date, RightAlly may suspend access until it is paid. Suspension does not reduce your obligations.

7. Early termination

If you end the agreement before the minimum term, the monthly fees for the remaining months become due immediately.

8. No refunds

Fees paid are non-refundable, except that if RightAlly does not deliver the implementation within 60 days of signing for reasons solely within its control, you may terminate and receive a refund of implementation fee amounts paid.

9–13. Responsibilities, data, intellectual property, warranties, liability

Full clauses as approved in the design pack.

14. Governing law and venue

Florida law applies. Disputes are heard in the courts of Miami-Dade County, Florida. Each party waives any right to a jury trial.

15. Electronic signatures

Both parties agree to sign electronically under the federal E-SIGN Act and Fla. Stat. § 668.50.
