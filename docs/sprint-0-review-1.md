# Sprint 0, review round 1 (changes)

- Onboarding asks for the number of agents; the monthly fee updates live. Fewer than 5 is accepted and billed at the 5-agent minimum.
- `?coupon=CODE` in the onboarding link pre-fills and applies the coupon. Expired or unknown codes show a clear message.
- RightAlly's signature is applied automatically from Settings (name in signature font by default, or an uploaded image).
- Coupons: edit, extend, "Never expires", active/off, copy share link. Used codes can't be renamed.
- Every admin menu and tab works: customer detail tabs, contracts (signed, renewals, templates), invoices (paid, failed, upcoming), admins and roles.
- Customer detail now highlights Customers in the menu.
- New Email templates menu: 16 automatic emails (edit, CC on/off, switch off, reset, send test) plus custom templates for one-off emails from a customer page, with placeholders and live preview.
